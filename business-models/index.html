FAQ

Questions about who we help

Which business models do you specialise in?

+

D2C brands, B2B businesses, e-commerce and Shopify stores, and product businesses — plus local service businesses in Kerala.

Why does business model matter for marketing?

+

Each model buys differently. D2C depends on acquisition cost and repeat purchase, B2B on lead quality and sales cycles, e-commerce on product discovery and conversion. Strategy should follow how your customers buy.

Do you work with small businesses?

+

Yes. Many of our clients are small and growing businesses. Plans are scoped to fit your goals and stage.

Which markets do you serve?

+

Kerala, India, the UAE, Bahrain and the wider GCC.

Can you help a Kerala business expand to the GCC?

+

Yes. We plan localised campaigns, landing pages and messaging for customers in the UAE and Bahrain.

What if my business fits more than one model?

+

That's common — for example a brand selling both D2C and wholesale. We plan each journey separately and connect them in one measurement framework.

What industries do you work with?

+

Healthcare and clinics, Ayurveda, restaurants and hospitality, real estate, retail and jewellery, boutiques and textiles, education, and D2C consumer brands.

How much does it cost to work with Billion Conversion?

+

Every engagement is custom-quoted. The price depends on your goals, the channels involved, the markets you target, the amount of content or development needed and your separate advertising budget. Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form for a quote.
