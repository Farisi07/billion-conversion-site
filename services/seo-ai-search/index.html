FAQ

Questions about seo, aeo & geo

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

+

SEO improves visibility in search engine results. AEO structures content to directly answer questions. GEO helps generative AI tools understand, trust and cite your business. They share the same foundation: helpful content on a technically sound site.

Can you guarantee a #1 ranking or a ChatGPT mention?

+

No. Nobody controls Google's rankings or AI answers. We improve the factors you can control and measure visibility honestly.

How long does SEO take?

+

Technical fixes can show impact within weeks; competitive rankings usually take several months of consistent work.

How do I appear on Google Maps in Kozhikode?

+

Create and verify a Google Business Profile at a genuine, staffed address, choose accurate categories and services, add photos, collect genuine reviews and keep your name, address and phone consistent across your website and listings.

How do I get my business mentioned in ChatGPT or Google AI Overviews?

+

There is no special switch. Make sure your site can be crawled (including OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot), keep business information consistent, publish clear answers to real questions and share original evidence such as case studies. Inclusion is never guaranteed.

Do I need special schema or an llms.txt file for AI search?

+

Google says no special markup is required for AI Overviews or AI Mode; normal SEO best practice applies. Accurate structured data that matches your visible content is still useful.

Is technical SEO still important?

+

Yes. If search engines cannot crawl, render or index your pages efficiently, content quality cannot help. Technical SEO covers crawlability, speed, structured data, canonicals and internal links.

How do you measure AI search visibility?

+

We track AI referral traffic in Google Analytics (for example visits tagged utm_source=chatgpt.com), Search Console data for AI features, and a documented set of prompt checks as a directional signal.
