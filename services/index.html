FAQ

Questions about our services

What services does Billion Conversion offer?

+

We offer growth marketing, performance marketing (Google, Meta and Click-to-WhatsApp ads), SEO, AEO and GEO, conversion rate optimisation, website development, Shopify development, e-commerce marketing and social media marketing.

Can I hire you for just one service?

+

Yes. You can start with a single service, such as Google Ads or a new website, or combine several into one growth plan.

Which service should I start with?

+

It depends on where growth is blocked. If you have little traffic, start with search or paid acquisition. If you have traffic but few enquiries, start with conversion optimisation. We recommend a starting point after a short discovery call.

Do you build websites as well as run marketing?

+

Yes. We design and develop business websites, landing pages and Shopify stores, and connect them to SEO, ads and analytics so they are built to generate enquiries.

Do you offer local SEO for Kozhikode businesses?

+

Yes. Local SEO includes Google Business Profile optimisation, local landing pages, review processes and Google Maps visibility for businesses in Kozhikode, Calicut and across Kerala.

How do you report on results?

+

We provide plain-language reports focused on leads, cost per lead, conversion rate and revenue where data allows, alongside channel metrics such as clicks and impressions.

How much does it cost to work with Billion Conversion?

+

Every engagement is custom-quoted. The price depends on your goals, the channels involved, the markets you target, the amount of content or development needed and your separate advertising budget. Message us on WhatsApp or use the contact form for a quote.

Do you work with businesses outside India?

+

Yes. We serve businesses in the UAE, Bahrain and the wider GCC, with calls scheduled in their time zone.
