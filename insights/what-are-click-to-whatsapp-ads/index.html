What are Click-to-WhatsApp ads, and when should you use them?

By Billion Conversion team · Published 15 Nov 2026 · 5 min read

Short answer

Click-to-WhatsApp ads are Facebook and Instagram ads that open a WhatsApp chat with your business instead of a website. They work well when customers want to ask a question before they buy or book — common for clinics, real estate, education, retail and services in Kerala and the GCC.

How they work

You run an ad on Facebook or Instagram with a “Send WhatsApp message” button. When someone taps it, WhatsApp opens with a pre-filled message to your business number. Your team (or an automated greeting) replies and continues the conversation.

When they work best

  • Your product or service needs a conversation before purchase (price, availability, appointments).
  • Your customers already use WhatsApp to contact businesses.
  • You can reply quickly — speed of response strongly affects results.

When a website or form may be better

  • You need detailed qualification information before a call.
  • You sell many products online and want people to browse and buy directly.
  • You don't have someone available to reply to chats promptly.

How to measure them

  1. Track conversations started in Meta Ads Manager.
  2. Use WhatsApp Business labels (for example New lead, Quoted, Won, Lost) so you can count qualified leads and sales, not just chats.
  3. Use distinct pre-filled messages per campaign so you know which ad each lead came from.
  4. Review cost per conversation and cost per qualified lead every week.

FAQ

Related questions

What are Click-to-WhatsApp ads?

+

Facebook and Instagram ads with a button that opens a WhatsApp chat with your business instead of a website.

Which businesses benefit most from Click-to-WhatsApp ads?

+

Businesses where customers want to ask before buying or booking — clinics, real estate, education, retail, services and restaurants.

Do I need the WhatsApp Business app?

+

You need a WhatsApp Business account (app or platform) connected to your Facebook page or Meta Business account.

Are Click-to-WhatsApp ads better than lead forms?

+

Not always. They are better when conversation drives sales; lead forms or websites can be better when you need detailed information or online checkout.

How fast should I reply to WhatsApp leads?

+

As fast as possible. Response speed strongly affects whether a chat becomes a customer, so set up greetings, quick replies and staff availability.

How do I measure Click-to-WhatsApp ads?

+

Track conversations started in Meta Ads Manager, label chats in WhatsApp Business (New, Quoted, Won, Lost) and review cost per qualified lead.

Can I know which ad a WhatsApp lead came from?

+

Yes, by using a different pre-filled message for each campaign or ad.

Do Click-to-WhatsApp ads work in the UAE and Bahrain?

+

Yes. WhatsApp is widely used across the GCC, and the same ad format is available.
