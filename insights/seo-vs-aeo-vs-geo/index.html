SEO vs AEO vs GEO: what's the difference, and what should a business do?

By Billion Conversion team · Published 15 Nov 2026 · 6 min read

Short answer

SEO helps your pages rank in search results. AEO structures content so search engines can lift a direct answer to a question. GEO helps AI tools such as Google AI Overviews and ChatGPT understand, trust and cite your business. All three rest on the same foundation: a crawlable website, genuinely useful content and consistent business information.

The short version

SEOAEOGEO
GoalRank pages in search resultsBe the direct answer to a questionBe understood and cited by AI-generated answers
Where it showsGoogle and Bing results, Google MapsFeatured answers, “People also ask”, voiceGoogle AI Overviews and AI Mode, ChatGPT search, Perplexity, Gemini
Main leversTechnical health, relevance, links, local signalsClear questions and concise answers, structureCrawler access, entity consistency, original evidence

What Google says about AI features

Google's guidance for site owners says there are no special requirements or special markup needed to appear in AI Overviews or AI Mode: a page needs to be indexed and eligible to show a snippet, and normal SEO best practice applies. Traffic from these features is reported inside Search Console's normal Performance report.

What to do in practice

  1. Make sure you can be crawled. Check robots.txt and any CDN or firewall rules so that Googlebot, Bingbot and the AI search crawlers you want (for example OpenAI's OAI-SearchBot) are not blocked.
  2. Keep business information consistent. Use the same brand name, services, contact details and locations on your website, Google Business Profile and social profiles.
  3. Answer real questions. Write pages that answer what buyers ask — cost, process, timelines, comparisons — with a direct answer near the top, followed by detail.
  4. Publish original evidence. Case studies, data and expert explanations are more likely to be referenced than generic content.
  5. Measure. Track Search Console queries, Google Business Profile actions and AI referrals (ChatGPT adds utm_source=chatgpt.com to links it sends).

What not to do

  • Don't mass-produce thin pages for every keyword or location.
  • Don't add structured data that doesn't match the visible page.
  • Be wary of anyone who guarantees rankings or AI mentions — no one controls them.

FAQ

Related questions

What is the difference between SEO, AEO and GEO?

+

SEO helps pages rank in search results, AEO structures content to be shown as a direct answer, and GEO helps generative AI tools understand and cite your business.

Is GEO replacing SEO?

+

No. AI search features rely on the same foundations as SEO: crawlable pages, helpful content and trustworthy information. GEO builds on SEO rather than replacing it.

What is answer engine optimisation (AEO)?

+

AEO is writing and structuring content so that it directly answers specific questions, with a clear answer near the top, descriptive headings, lists and tables.

What is generative engine optimisation (GEO)?

+

GEO is improving how AI systems such as Google AI Overviews, ChatGPT search, Perplexity and Gemini understand, trust and reference your business.

Does Google require special markup for AI Overviews?

+

No. Google states there are no additional requirements; a page must be indexed and eligible to show a snippet, and normal SEO best practice applies.

Should I block AI crawlers?

+

Only if you don't want your content used by those systems. If you want visibility in AI search, allow crawlers such as Googlebot and OAI-SearchBot.

How can I track traffic from ChatGPT?

+

ChatGPT adds utm_source=chatgpt.com to links it sends, so you can see those visits in Google Analytics.

Can anyone guarantee I'll appear in AI answers?

+

No. AI answers vary by query and change over time. You can improve the factors you control and measure the results.
