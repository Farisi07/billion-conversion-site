Privacy Policy

How Billion Conversion — operated by Billiyon Conversion LLP — collects, uses and protects your personal data.

17 November 2026
Last updated

Website

1. Who We Are

This website is operated by Billiyon Conversion LLP (LLPIN ADD-1911), doing business as Billion Conversion (“Billion Conversion”, “we”, “our” or “us”).

For the personal data described in this policy, Billiyon Conversion LLP is the data fiduciary (also called the data controller): we decide why and how that data is used.

  • Registered office: Mudapuram P.O., Attingal, Thiruvananthapuram, Kerala, India
  • Operational office: KINFRA Advanced Technology Park, Ramanattukara, Kozhikode, Kerala, India
  • Email: info@billionconversion.com

2. Scope of This Policy

This policy applies to personal data we handle when you:

  • visit and browse this website;
  • use our contact form;
  • contact us by WhatsApp, phone or email; or
  • interact with our official social media profiles and are redirected to this website.

Personal data we process while delivering services to clients (for example, access to a client's ad accounts or analytics) is governed by the agreement with that client, not by this policy.

3. Personal Data We Collect

3.1 Information you give us

  • Contact form: name, business name, phone or WhatsApp number, email address (optional), country, the service you are interested in and your message.
  • WhatsApp, phone and email: your name, number or email address, and anything you choose to tell us in the conversation, such as details about your business, goals and budget.

Please do not send us sensitive personal data (such as health, financial account or identity document details). We do not need it to answer an enquiry.

3.2 How the contact form works

Our contact form checks your details in your browser and then prepares a pre-written WhatsApp message. The form itself does not send your details to our servers or store them on this website. Your details reach us only when you press Send in WhatsApp. From that point, the message is handled under Section 3.1 and WhatsApp's own privacy policy.

3.3 Information collected automatically (only with your consent)

If you click Accept on our cookie banner, we use:

  • Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics 4 to measure visits and actions such as pages viewed, buttons clicked, form starts and WhatsApp clicks. This includes information such as device and browser type, approximate location (derived from your IP address), referring website and campaign tags in the page address (for example, utm_source).
  • Microsoft Clarity to produce heatmaps and session recordings that show how visitors scroll, click and move around pages, so we can find and fix problems. Clarity masks the text you type into form fields.

If you click Decline, or have not made a choice yet, these tools run in a restricted, cookieless mode: they do not set analytics or advertising cookies and do not link your visits together. Our analytics events never include your name, phone number, email address or message.

3.4 Technical data

Like all websites, our hosting providers automatically receive technical information needed to deliver pages securely, such as your IP address, browser type and the time of the request. This is used for security and reliability.

4. Why We Use Your Data and Our Legal Basis

  • Responding to your enquiry and preparing a quotation — based on your consent and your request to be contacted, which you give when you tick the consent box on the form or message us yourself.
  • Following up about your enquiry by WhatsApp, phone or email — based on your consent. You can ask us to stop at any time.
  • Analytics and website improvement using Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity — based on your consent through the cookie banner.
  • Website security, fraud and spam prevention — necessary to operate the website safely (a legitimate use).
  • Meeting legal obligations, such as keeping business and tax records or responding to lawful requests from authorities.

We do not use your data for automated decisions that have legal or similarly significant effects on you.

5. Cookies and Similar Technologies

We use a small number of technologies, grouped as follows:

  • Strictly necessary (always on): your browser's storage remembers your cookie choice and whether you have already seen the opening animation in this session. These do not track you.
  • Analytics (only after you accept): cookies set by Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, used as described in Section 3.3.

We do not currently use advertising or remarketing cookies on this website. If we add them, we will update this policy and ask for your consent first.

Changing your choice: clear this website's data in your browser settings and reload the page. The cookie banner will appear again. You can also block cookies in your browser, or install the Google Analytics opt-out add-on.

6. Who We Share Data With

We do not sell or rent your personal data. We share it only with service providers that help us run the website and our business:

  • WhatsApp (Meta Platforms) — messaging, when you contact us on WhatsApp.
  • Google — Google Tag Manager and Google Analytics.
  • Our email service provider — to send and receive email at info@billionconversion.com.
  • Microsoft — Microsoft Clarity.
  • Website hosting providers — to serve this website securely.

These providers process data under their own terms and privacy policies. We may also disclose personal data if required by law, court order or a lawful request from a government authority, or to protect our rights, users or the public. If our business is restructured, merged or sold, personal data may be transferred to the new owner under the same protections.

7. International Transfers

We are based in India and serve clients in India, the UAE, Bahrain and the wider GCC. Some of our service providers (for example Google, Microsoft and Meta) store or process data on servers outside your country, including in the United States. Where we transfer personal data internationally, we rely on providers that apply recognised safeguards and do so only as permitted by applicable law.

8. How Long We Keep Data

  • Enquiries that do not become projects: up to 24 months after our last contact, so we can refer back to the conversation if you get in touch again. You can ask us to delete it sooner.
  • Client records: for the duration of the engagement and afterwards for as long as required by law for accounting, tax and contractual records.
  • Analytics data: according to the retention settings of Google Analytics and Microsoft Clarity, which we keep at the shortest periods that still let us compare performance over time.

When data is no longer needed, we delete it or anonymise it.

9. How We Protect Your Data

We use reasonable technical and organisational safeguards, including encrypted connections (HTTPS) for this website, access to business accounts limited to people who need it, and keeping enquiry data only as long as needed. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, but we work to protect your data and will act promptly if a breach occurs, including notifying affected people and authorities where the law requires it.

10. Your Rights

Depending on the laws that apply to you, you can ask us to:

  • confirm whether we hold your personal data and give you a summary of it and how we use it;
  • correct, complete or update inaccurate or incomplete data;
  • delete your data, where we no longer need it or you withdraw consent;
  • withdraw your consent at any time — this does not affect anything we did before you withdrew it;
  • stop sending you follow-up messages;
  • nominate another person to exercise your rights if you die or become unable to do so (under India's DPDP Act); and
  • raise a grievance with us about how your data has been handled.

To make a request, contact us using the details in Section 13. We may need to confirm your identity before acting on it. We aim to respond within 30 days. We will not treat you differently for exercising your rights.

11. Grievances and Complaints

If you have a concern about how we handle your personal data, please contact our grievance contact first, Salmanul Faris B (Founder), at info@billionconversion.com. We will acknowledge your grievance and aim to resolve it within 30 days.

If you are not satisfied with our response, you may complain to the relevant authority. In India this is the Data Protection Board of India. In the UAE and Bahrain, it is the data protection authority that applies to you.

12. Children

This website and our services are intended for businesses and adults. We do not knowingly collect personal data from anyone under 18. If you believe a child has sent us personal data, please contact us and we will delete it.

15. Changes to This Policy

We may update this policy when our practices, tools or the law change. The “Last updated” date at the top shows when it last changed. If we make a significant change, such as using your data for a new purpose, we will highlight it on this website and ask for your consent where required.
