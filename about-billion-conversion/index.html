About Billion Conversion

Who is Billion Conversion?

Billion Conversion is a full-service growth marketing agency based in Kerala, helping businesses scale through data-driven digital marketing, performance advertising, SEO and conversion rate optimisation (CRO). We combine strategy, creativity, technology and analytics to turn online visibility into measurable business growth.

Company introduction

Billion Conversion at a glance

Billion Conversion is a growth marketing agency based in Kerala, India. It helps businesses scale through digital marketing, performance marketing, SEO and conversion-focused strategies, and measures its work by qualified leads, customers and revenue.

From building high-converting websites and Shopify stores to managing Google Ads, Meta Ads, social media marketing, SEO and AI-powered creative production, we help brands attract the right audience, generate qualified leads and increase revenue.

Our approach goes beyond clicks and impressions. We focus on what actually matters to businesses: leads, conversions, customer acquisition and sustainable growth.

We serve businesses across Kerala, India, the UAE, Bahrain and the wider GCC.

Company history

How Billion Conversion started

Billion Conversion was founded in 2026 by Salmanul Faris B with a simple idea: marketing should be judged by the numbers that matter to a business — leads, customers and revenue — not by likes and impressions. That idea became the company's tagline, “We Turn Growth Into Numbers.”

The business is registered as Billiyon Conversion LLP and operates from KINFRA Advanced Technology Park in Ramanattukara, Kozhikode, Kerala. From Kerala, Billion Conversion works with businesses in India, the UAE, Bahrain and the wider GCC, bringing search, paid media, websites and conversion optimisation together as one connected growth system.
